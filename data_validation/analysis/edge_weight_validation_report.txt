
======================================================================
OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Is mean(total) per sw edge (platform -> platform) a trustworthy static weight for shortest-path algorithms on the weighted graph? We test this two ways:
  1. overall variability (stdev/CV) - is the spread small relative to the mean, regardless of cause?
  2. time-of-day structure (hourly range) - does the mean hide a rush/off-peak pattern a static weight would wash out?
The edge weight itself stays total ('arrival_b - arrival_a'). Every flagged edge also gets it split into door ('departure_a - arrival_a', dwell at the boarding platform) and sw ('arrival_b - departure_a', time spent actually moving between platforms), as a diagnostic for *why* it's noisy, not as a third thing being proven.
